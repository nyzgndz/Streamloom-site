StreamLoom

A media player for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad that plays your own playlists.

StreamLoom plays live channels, movies and series from a playlist you add: an Xtream Codes login or an M3U Playlist link from a service you have the right to use.

StreamLoom comes with no channels, playlists or content of any kind, and does not sell or recommend any provider. It is not affiliated with any provider. You are responsible for what you add and watch, and the developer does not support watching anything without the permission of its owner.

One purchase works on all three. Your playlists stay on your device. StreamLoom has no account, no analytics and no tracking.
