Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

StreamLoom is a media player for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad, made and sold by Niyazi Gunduz, an individual developer in Finland (mystreamloom@gmail.com).

The short version

StreamLoom collects no personal data. It has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. What you add to StreamLoom stays on the device you add it on.

What stays on your device

  • The Playlists you add: the server address, username and password (kept in the device’s Keychain, set to “this device only”), or the playlist link.
  • The channel, movie and series lists and the TV guide your provider sends, kept as a cache that the app can rebuild.
  • Your favorites, watch progress and recently watched items, and your settings.
  • Your PIN, if you set one (in the Keychain, this device only), and which categories it locks.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is not synced through iCloud. Removing a Playlist in StreamLoom removes its data, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Who StreamLoom talks to

  • Your provider, at the address you entered: to sign in, download the lists and guide, and play streams. What your provider does with your data is covered by its own terms and privacy policy.
  • The addresses your Playlist gives for pictures (posters, channel logos). Those servers see a request from your device, as any website does.
  • Apple, for the App Store purchase and for checking it. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the developer receives no personal details from it, only the anonymous sales totals every App Store developer sees.

If an address starts with http:// and not https://, what is sent and received is not encrypted; the app warns about this when you enter such an address.

What the developer receives

Nothing from the app. If you write to the contact email, the developer sees what you send and uses it only to answer you; the message is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Children

StreamLoom is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to look up, correct or delete on the developer’s side; your data on your device is under your control. You can still write to the contact email with any question, and you can complain to the Finnish data protection authority (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto) or the one where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date. If the app ever began to collect data, it would tell you and ask first.
