Support

Write to mystreamloom@gmail.com. Tell us your device (Apple TV, iPhone or iPad, and its model), its software version (tvOS, iOS or iPadOS) and what happened. Please do not send your playlist link, username or password: they are not needed and should stay private.

Adding a playlist

Choose Add Playlist, then Xtream Codes if your provider gave you a server address, username and password, or M3U Playlist if it gave you a link. Include the port in the server address if your provider gave you one.

A channel or film does not play

StreamLoom plays what your provider sends. If something does not play, check with your provider first that your subscription is active and that the stream works. Many providers allow only one connection at a time: close StreamLoom on other devices.

Where can I find a playlist?

StreamLoom comes with no content and cannot help you find a playlist or a provider. Use a service you have the right to use.

Buying StreamLoom

You can try StreamLoom free for 7 days and then unlock it with a one-time purchase; nothing is charged automatically. The trial counts from the day you start it, also after reinstalling or on another device. The purchase is for the player only; it includes no channels, content or subscription, and with Family Sharing your family can use it too. Settings > Purchase shows the days left. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: see reportaproblem.apple.com. One purchase covers Apple TV, iPhone and iPad: on another device signed in with the same Apple Account, use Restore Purchases.

Locked categories and the PIN

Settings > PIN sets a four-digit PIN and chooses the categories it locks. Forgot the PIN? Remove the playlist in Settings > Playlists and add it again: that clears its locked categories, and once no playlist has any left, the PIN can be changed or turned off without the old one. The PIN is kept only on the device, so the developer cannot reset it.

For owners of content

StreamLoom hosts, lists and links to no content, so content has to be taken down where it is offered, by the provider. If you believe StreamLoom is being used to reach your content without permission, or that something the developer published uses your material, write to mystreamloom@gmail.com with who you are and the rights you hold, what the content is, where it is offered, and why you believe it is not authorised. Every such message is read and answered.
